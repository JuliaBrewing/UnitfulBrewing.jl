Dimensions and units

Dimensions

UnitfulBrewing adds the following dimensions to the ones in Unitful:

DimensionQuantityReference unit
𝐒𝐂Sugar contents°P
𝐂𝐋ColorSRM
𝐁𝐓BitternessIBU
𝐃𝐏Diastatic power°Lintner
𝐂𝐁Carbonationvols

Each one is a dimension of its own, so, for instance, a color cannot be accidentally added to a bitterness or converted to a plain number.

Sugar contents and gravity

UnitNameDefinition
°Pdegree Platoreference unit for 𝐒𝐂
Brixdegree Brixcurrently equal to °P
Ballingdegree Ballingcurrently equal to °P
sgspecific gravitydimensionless, 1 sg = 1
gugravity unitaffine: gu = 1000 (sg - 1)
gpgravity pointalias of gu

A specific gravity of 1.040 is 40 gravity units:

julia> uconvert(u"gu", 1.040u"sg")
40.0 gu

julia> uconvert(u"sg", 48u"gu")
1.048 sg

Sugar contents and specific gravity have different dimensions, so converting between them requires one of the equivalences SugarGravity or SugarGravityQuad; see Equivalences.

Color

UnitNameDefinition
SRMStandard Reference Methodreference unit for 𝐂𝐋
EBCEuropean Brewery ConventionEBC = 1.97 SRM
°Ldegree Lovibondaffine: SRM = 1.3546 °L - 0.76

srm, ebc, Lovi and Lovibond are aliases of SRM, EBC and °L.

julia> uconvert(u"EBC", 10u"SRM")
197//10 EBC

julia> round(u"°L", 20u"SRM", digits=2)
15.33 °L

Carbonation

UnitNameDefinition
volsvolumes of CO₂reference unit for 𝐂𝐁: volumes of CO₂, at 0 °C and 1 atm, dissolved per volume of beer

vols is the unit used for carbonation by BeerJSON. Use the equivalence CarbonationDensity to convert to and from the mass of CO₂ per volume, in g/l.

Concentration

Unitful (since v1.19) already defines percent, permille, pertenthousand, pcm, ppm, ppb, ppt and ppq, which are used here as they are. Use the equivalence DensityConcentration to convert between these and mass per volume, such as mg/l.

pH

UnitNameDefinition
pH⁺power of hydrogenlogarithmic unit, base 10
pwrHpower of hydrogenalias of pH⁺

The usual symbol pH cannot be used, since Unitful already defines pH as the picohenry, a unit of electrical inductance.

julia> 3u"pH⁺" < 5u"pwrH"
true
